Block Stack — Privacy Policy

Developer: packet-chan
Last updated: 25 October 2026
Android package: com.packetchan.SteslthXO

This policy applies to Block Stack version 3.0 and later, the replacement for Ink Drop - 脳トレ物理パズル.

Game data

Your best score and haptic preference are saved in app preferences on your device. We do not operate an account system or a server that receives your game progress. Device backup services may include these preferences according to your operating system and backup settings. You can clear this data through Android app settings or by deleting the app.

Advertising and third-party data

The Android app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to offer an optional rewarded video. Google may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising and other device identifiers, app interactions, crash information, diagnostics and performance information. These are used for advertising, analytics related to advertising, fraud prevention and security. SDK data is encrypted in transit. Google's retention and processing practices are described in its policies.

We use Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent and offer privacy choices where required. Where available, use “Ad privacy settings” in the app to change your choices. You can also manage or reset your advertising ID in your device settings. Declining or not completing an ad does not prevent ordinary gameplay; an extra continue is granted only when the ad reports a completed reward.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information for advertising

Permissions and contact

The game uses internet access for ads and vibration for optional haptic feedback. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. It does not offer account creation or collect a name or email address in the game.

For privacy questions or requests, contact packetchan21@gmail.com. If you email us, we use the information you provide to respond to your request. We may update this policy when the app changes and will update the date above.
